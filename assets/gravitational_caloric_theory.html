Research overview

A vector field coupled to matter

Gravitational caloric theory extends general relativity by introducing a vector field sourced by and coupled to the fluid sector.

The original motivation was to use the cosmic radiation-matter transition to trigger early dark energy.

Early dark energy remains a controversial candidate approach to the Hubble tension.
